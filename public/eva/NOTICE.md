# Surface terrain assets — attribution

The files under `public/eva/` are reused from **Access Mars**
(https://github.com/googlecreativelab/access-mars), © 2017 Google Inc.,
released under the Apache License 2.0 (copy in `LICENSE-access-mars.txt`).

* Terrain meshes and tile textures were reconstructed by NASA/JPL-Caltech from
  images taken by the Curiosity rover (Gale crater) and packaged by Google
  Creative Lab with NASA JPL Ops Lab.
* Point-of-interest texts and card images (`cards/`) come from the same project.
* Changes in MARS // SURVIVE: only a subset of the original files is included
  (four sites, `_xsm`/`_sm`/base texture levels, no `_lg` levels, no audio).
  Files are otherwise unmodified.
